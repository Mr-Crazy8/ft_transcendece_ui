## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an authoritative, intellectual aesthetic tailored for verified professional discourse and deep knowledge exchange. Eschewing the superficial engagement loops, vanity metrics, and noise of conventional social platforms, the interface projects gravitas, academic clarity, and focused substance.

### Design Movement
- **Editorial Modernism meets Dark Precision**: Rooted in high-end editorial book design combined with technical SaaS execution.
- High-contrast hierarchy anchored by deep, immersive abyssal charcoal backdrops and crisp, highly legible typography.
- Deliberate quietude: Generous negative space gives ideas breathing room, treating thought pieces and peer evaluations with literary respect.
- Signals of validation: The **Verified Pulse glow** provides subtle, non-distracting luminescent proof of authenticity without resorting to loud gamified badges.

## Colors

The palette is engineered exclusively for a dark, focused workspace that minimises visual fatigue during prolonged reading and cognitive analysis.

- **Primary Accent (`#6C63FF` - Electric Indigo)**: Used for primary calls-to-action, active stage indications, and focused interactive inputs.
- **Secondary Accent (`#9B8BFF` - Soft Lavender)**: Expresses secondary guidance, supporting data points, badge borders, and progressive gradient endpoints.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#38E8A0` - Verified Emerald)**: Reserved specifically for authentication indicators, status confirmation dots, and the subtle "Verified Pulse" ring.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces**:
  - `Canvas Canvas`: `#0E0F13` — The grounding deep charcoal space.
  - `Surface Low`: `#14151C` — Subdued containers, sunken text areas, and inputs.
  - `Surface High`: `#1A1B22` — Card surfaces, modal sheets, and active panels.
  - `Surface Border`: `#2A2B35` — Crisp, low-luminance divider lines and component outlines.
  - `Text Primary`: `#F4F4F7` — High-contrast off-white for headers and active text.
  - `Text Secondary`: `#9E9EA7` — Medium-contrast muted gray for body discourse.
  - `Text Muted`: `#5D5E6B` — Low-contrast metadata, sequence counters, and decorative prefixes.

## Typography

Typography balances authoritative editorial tradition with technological utility.

- **Headline Hierarchy (Newsreader)**: Evokes the scholarly weight of academic publishing and literary journalism. Used for high-level concepts, narrative milestones, and section headings. It should always retain its optical character and unforced natural letter spacing.
- **Functional & Body Copy (Inter)**: Provides pristine legibility for long-form argumentation, input states, and tabular user data.
- **Eyebrow & Section Indexing**: Micro-headers, breadcrumbs, and question indexing (e.g., `01 / VERIFY YOUR EXPERTISE`) must render in uppercase Inter with extended tracking (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) and subdued tone (`#5D5E6B`).

## Layout & Spacing

The design system enforces a strict 8px incremental baseline rhythm to align editorial blocks with surgical alignment.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: 1200px maximum container width for standard flows; 840px constrained reading column for single-topic deliberation and verification prompts. Margins: 64px (`margin-desktop`). Gutters: 32px (`gutter-lg`).
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Fluid down to 768px. Margins: 32px (`margin`). Gutters: 24px (`gutter`).
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: Below 768px, layouts stack linearly into single-column modules. Margins: 20px (`margin-mobile`). Gutters: 16px (`gutter-sm`).

### Rhythm Rules
- Asymmetrical split layouts place contextual progression guides on the left (e.g., 4 columns) and primary work canvases on the right (e.g., 8 columns).
- Padding inside interactive containers follows a standard `space-lg` (24px) for cards and `space-xl` (40px) for prominent assessment modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows or skeuomorphic bevels; it is defined by delicate tonal layering and sharp structural boundaries.

### Surfaces & Tonal Depth
- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: `#0E0F13` serves as the bedrock canvas.
- **Level 1 (Submerged/Inputs)**: `#14151C` with border `1px solid #2A2B35`.
- **Level 2 (Cards & Modules)**: `#1A1B22` framed by subtle border `1px solid #2A2B35`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Overlays/Dropdowns)**: `#21222C` with border `1px solid #363746` and diffused ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.

### Verified Pulse Glow Indicator
- Used to signify active system intelligence or real-time expertise validation.
- Implemented as an ambient, tinted box-shadow/filter: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px 2px rgba(108, 99, 255, 0.25)`.
- For Verified status badges: An inner-glow accent using emerald tint `0 0 10px rgba(56, 232, 160, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a disciplined geometry with uniform 10px corner radiuses across all primary interactive surfaces, delivering an architectural, composed appearance.

- **Cards & Modal Containers**: `10px` (`rounded-lg` token calibrated to 10px).
- **Inputs & Button Actions**: `8px` to `10px` to mirror surface geometry seamlessly.
- **Pills & Status Chips**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) reserved strictly for compact metadata tokens, progress pills, and the verification status indicator.
- **Line Accents**: 1px crisp horizontal hairline rules (`#2A2B35`) demarcate headers and footers across the 100% viewport width.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#6C63FF` fill, white typography (`Inter`, 14px, medium), 10px border radius, subtle hover elevation (`#7F77FF`). Free of harsh outer drop shadows.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent background with a `1px solid #2A2B35` border and `#F4F4F7` text. On hover, background shifts to `#1A1B22` and border brightens to `#3E404F`.
- **Destructive / Alert**: Subtle deep crimson outline with low-saturation text to maintain overall brand composure.

### Input Fields & Textareas
- Deep slate background (`#14151C`) enclosed by `1px solid #2A2B35`.
- Placeholder text: Muted gray (`#5D5E6B`).
- Focus state: Border shifts to `#6C63FF` accompanied by a soft glow (`0 0 0 2px rgba(108, 99, 255, 0.2)`). No default browser outlines.

### Cards & Modular Containers
- Constructed with `#1A1B22` surface fills and `1px solid #2A2B35` boundary borders.
- Padding scales from 24px (standard metrics and quote cards) to 36px (complex verification interview cards).
- Interactive cards elevate border radiance to `#3F404E` on hover without translation jumps.

### Chips & Verification Badges
- Pill format with an ultra-subtle backdrop tint: `rgba(108, 99, 255, 0.1)` with a `1px solid rgba(108, 99, 255, 0.3)` perimeter.
- Status indicator variants (e.g., *Verification Pending*, *Verified Fellow*) integrate a 6px status dot illuminated with the Verified Pulse glow.

### Stepper & Progress Indexing
- Vertical and horizontal stage track with active node indicators. Active steps use crisp `#F4F4F7` text; future stages display muted `#5D5E6B`.
- Integrated progress bars feature a high-precision 2px to 3px gradient line flowing from Electric Indigo (`#6C63FF`) to Lavender (`#9B8BFF`).
